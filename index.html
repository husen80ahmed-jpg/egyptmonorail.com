Network

Two lines. Two different operating stages.

East Nile is operational. West Nile remains under development and is currently scheduled to enter service in 2027.

LIVE Verified

East Nile Monorail

Cairo Stadium in Nasr City to Justice City in the New Administrative Capital. The line spans 56.5 km and includes 22 stations, serving Nasr City, New Cairo and the New Capital.

Official NAT source ↗
UNDER DEVELOPMENT 2027 target

West Nile Monorail

New October to Wadi El Nile in Mohandessin. The current official route is approximately 43.8 km with 13 stations, including Mall of Egypt and Sheikh Zayed City.

Station intelligence

The useful question is not only: “Where is the station?”

The next layer is understanding what surrounds each station: residential developments, offices, retail, hospitals, universities, business districts and major destinations.

The objective is to build station profiles based on verified geography rather than vague claims such as “close to the monorail.”

Commercial catchments will be developed around approximately 500 metres, 1 kilometre and 2 kilometres where reliable location data is available.
