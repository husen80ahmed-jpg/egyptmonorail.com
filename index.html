Two lines. Two different operating stages.

East Nile is in commercial operation. West Nile remains under development, with operation currently indicated for 2027.

East Nile Monorail

The East Nile line connects Cairo Stadium in Nasr City with Justice City in the New Administrative Capital. Current official figures list 56.5 km and 22 stations.

Frequently asked questions

About EgyptMonorail.com

Is EgyptMonorail.com an official government website?

No. EgyptMonorail.com is an independent, non-official information and commercial-discovery platform.

Is the East Nile Monorail operational?

Yes. The East Nile Monorail is in commercial operation across its 22-station route. Operating information should still be rechecked against official sources before travel.

Can I use EgyptMonorail.com to find property near stations?

The site is developing a station-linked property discovery layer for New Cairo, the New Administrative Capital, October and Sheikh Zayed. Property information must be verified before any transaction.

Does EgyptMonorail.com sell official advertising inventory?

No. EgyptMonorail.com currently provides independent advertising market information and measures advertiser demand. It does not claim ownership of official monorail advertising inventory.
